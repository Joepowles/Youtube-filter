How this works

YouTube doesn't let parents block a channel on a regular account. This website does the next best thing: it stays signed in to each child's YouTube and does, for you, everything YouTube allows to push a channel away.

When you block a channel, it can:

  1. Remove every video from it in your child's watch history, so it stops shaping their suggestions.
  2. Unsubscribe from it.
  3. Remove their likes on its videos.
  4. Give its newest videos a thumbs down.
  5. Tell YouTube "Don't recommend this channel" on their homepage.

Good to know

  • Shorts: YouTube has no switch to turn Shorts off. "Hide Shorts" (in each child's Settings) hides the Shorts shelf on their homepage and removes Shorts from their watch history so YouTube suggests fewer of them. YouTube brings the shelf back after about 30 days, so the website hides it again every few hours. Shorts can still be reached through the Shorts button or by searching.
  • Your child can still find the channel by searching for it. If that happens and "Keep blocked channels cleaned up" is on (in each child's Settings), the website notices within a few minutes and cleans up again.
  • If a step shows a warning, tap Run again later. YouTube sometimes doesn't show the button the website needs.
  • For a child under 13 with a Google Family Link account, you can also block a channel outright in the Family Link app. That is stronger, and this website handles everything Family Link doesn't.
  • Everything runs on our own server, so you can close this page while it works.
